1. About these terms

These terms and conditions apply to every journey booked with Global Venturers. Please read them carefully, as they form the contract between you and us.

“We”, “us” and “our” mean Creative Capsule Ltd, trading as Global Venturers, a company registered in England & Wales (company number 08023109), registered office 6 Mc Williams Close, Talbot Village BH12 5HP. “You” and “your” mean everyone named on the booking. The person who makes the booking (the “lead traveller”) must be at least 18 and agrees to these terms on behalf of everyone on the booking.

2. Making a booking

  • To book, complete the booking form and pay a deposit of 30% of the journey price for each traveller.
  • Your booking is confirmed, and the contract between us begins, when we send you written confirmation by email.
  • Please check your confirmation carefully and tell us straight away if anything is wrong, including names, which must match your passports.
  • Travellers under 18 must travel with a parent or guardian, and our journeys are designed for adults.
  • Each departure is limited to eight travellers. Places are confirmed in the order deposits are received.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are per person in pounds sterling (GBP), based on two people sharing a room. A private room is available for a supplement.
  • What is and isn’t included is shown on each journey page. International flights, travel insurance, food and drinks (unless stated), tips and personal spending are not included.
  • The balance is due 60 days before departure. If you book within 60 days of departure, the full price is due when you book.
  • If we don’t receive the balance on time, we may treat your booking as cancelled and apply the cancellation charges in section 4.
  • Once your booking is confirmed, we will not increase the price.

4. If you cancel

You can cancel at any time by emailing us. Your cancellation takes effect on the day we receive it.

  • Within 14 days of booking (and more than 60 days before departure): we refund your deposit in full.
  • More than 60 days before departure: you lose your deposit.
  • 60 to 31 days before departure: 50% of the journey price.
  • 30 to 15 days before departure: 75% of the journey price.
  • 14 days or less before departure: 100% of the journey price.

Your travel insurance may cover these charges, depending on your reason for cancelling. If you leave a journey early, or miss part of it, we cannot refund unused services.

5. Changes you make

  • If you would like to change something after booking, tell us as soon as possible. We will try to help, but cannot guarantee every change.
  • You may transfer your place to someone else, as long as we receive their details at least 30 days before departure. There is no fee for this.
  • Moving to a different departure date counts as a cancellation and a new booking, unless we agree otherwise.

6. If we change or cancel your journey

  • Our journeys need a minimum of six travellers. If this isn’t reached, we will tell you at least 60 days before departure and give you a full refund of everything you’ve paid, or the option to move to another departure.
  • Small changes can happen, for example a different hotel of a similar standard or a change to the order of the days. We will tell you about these as soon as we can.
  • If we have to make a significant change before departure, you can accept the change, choose another journey, or cancel and receive a full refund.
  • We will not pay compensation where a change or cancellation is caused by events beyond our control, such as extreme weather, natural disasters, war, terrorism, civil unrest, epidemics or government action. In those cases we will always do our best to find an alternative and keep you safe.
  • During the journey, your tour leader may adjust the itinerary for safety, weather or road conditions.

7. Travel insurance

Travel insurance is a condition of booking. Your policy must cover medical expenses, emergency repatriation and personal liability, and we strongly recommend it also covers cancellation. Please bring your policy details with you; your tour leader will ask for them at the start of the journey.

8. Passports, visas and health

  • You are responsible for having a valid passport, any visas and any vaccinations needed for your journey. Most passports should be valid for at least six months after you return.
  • Please check the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office travel advice, or your own government’s advice, before you travel.
  • Our journeys involve walking in medinas, some long drives and nights in desert camps. Please tell us before you book about any medical condition, mobility need or allergy that could affect your journey, so we can advise you.

9. On your journey

  • Group travel works best when everyone respects each other, local people and local customs. Your tour leader’s decisions on safety are final.
  • If someone’s behaviour puts others at risk or seriously disrupts the group, we may ask them to leave the journey, with no refund.
  • Please take care of your own belongings. We are not responsible for loss of or damage to personal items.

10. Photos and filming

We photograph and film our journeys and share the images with the group. We will always ask before using identifiable images of you in our marketing. If you would rather not be photographed, just let your tour leader know. See our privacy policy for more.

11. Offers and promotions

From time to time we may offer discounts, for example to our mailing list subscribers. Unless an offer says otherwise:

  • it applies to the journey price only, not to supplements, flights, insurance or extras;
  • it must be quoted when you book and cannot be added to an existing booking;
  • only one offer can be used per booking, and offers cannot be combined;
  • it is subject to availability, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be transferred;
  • we may change or withdraw an offer at any time, without affecting bookings already confirmed.

12. Our responsibility to you

We will make sure the journey you book is provided with reasonable skill and care. If any part of it is not provided as promised, we will put things right where we can and, where appropriate, offer a fair refund or compensation.

We are not responsible where a problem is caused by you, by a third party not connected with your journey, or by unusual and unforeseeable events beyond our control. Our liability for losses that are not personal injury, illness or death is limited to the price of your journey. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence.

13. Complaints

If something isn’t right during your journey, please tell your tour leader straight away so we can fix it there and then. If you are still unhappy, please write to us at contact@globalventurers.com within 28 days of your return, and we will reply as quickly as we can.

14. Law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the law of England & Wales, and any dispute will be dealt with by the courts of England & Wales. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you may also bring proceedings in your local courts.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Global Venturers collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website, make an enquiry, book a journey or travel with us. We take your privacy seriously and only use your information in the ways described here.

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we introduce new services or when the law changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

2. Who we are

Global Venturers is a trading name of Creative Capsule Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (company number 08023109), with its registered office at 6 Mc Williams Close, Talbot Village BH12 5HP.

Creative Capsule Ltd is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. This means we decide how and why your information is used. ICO registration number: [ZA000000].

Our journeys in Morocco are operated on the ground with our partner company, Moroccan Ventures, which acts on our instructions when handling your information.

3. What information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact and identity details: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country of residence and date of birth.
  • Booking details: the journey you book, room preferences, the names of the people travelling with you, and passport details where hotels, camps or authorities need them.
  • Health and dietary information: any dietary needs, allergies, medical conditions or mobility needs you choose to tell us, so we can look after you properly. This is “special category” information and we only use it with your consent and for this purpose.
  • Emergency contact: the name and number of someone we can contact if something goes wrong while you are travelling.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by our payment provider. We see confirmation that you have paid, but we do not store your full card details.
  • Your messages: emails, WhatsApp messages, enquiry forms and any feedback you send us.
  • Photos and video: images taken during our journeys (see section 6).
  • Marketing preferences: whether you have joined our mailing list.
  • Website information: technical information such as your browser, device and the pages you visit, collected through cookies (see section 14).

4. Why we use your information

UK data protection law requires a lawful reason for each use of your information. We rely on:

  • Performing our contract with you: to take and manage your booking, arrange accommodation, transport and guides, take payments and look after you during your journey.
  • Legal obligations: to keep financial records, meet tax rules and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interests: to answer enquiries, improve our journeys and website, keep our business secure and send you important information about a journey you have booked.
  • Consent: to send you marketing emails, use health information, and feature identifiable photos of you in our marketing. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

5. How we collect your information

  • Directly from you, when you fill in a form, book, email us, message us on WhatsApp or talk to us.
  • From the lead traveller, when someone books on behalf of a group. If you book for others, please make sure they are happy for you to share their details and have seen this policy.
  • From our partners and suppliers during your journey, for example if a hotel or guide tells us about something that affects your trip.
  • Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies when you use our website.

6. Photos and filming on our journeys

Our founder Bijan Paul is a photographer and filmmaker, and we capture our journeys in photos and video. We share these with the group so you have lasting memories.

We will always ask before using a photo or video in which you can be clearly identified on our website, social media or other marketing. If you would rather not be photographed, just tell your tour leader at the start of the journey and we will respect that.

7. Sharing your information

We only share what is needed, with:

  • Travel suppliers: hotels, riads, desert camps, drivers, local guides and our partner Moroccan Ventures, so they can provide your journey.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run our business, such as our payment provider, email and form providers, mailing list service, website hosting and accountants. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Emergency services and medical providers if you need help during your journey.
  • Authorities where the law requires it, for example police registration rules for hotels.
  • Your insurer if you make a claim and ask us to help.

8. We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

9. International transfers

Because our journeys take place abroad, some of your information must be shared with suppliers outside the UK, for example in Morocco. Where we transfer information outside the UK, we do so because it is necessary to provide the journey you have booked, or we put appropriate safeguards in place, such as contracts that require the recipient to protect your information to UK standards.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need it;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using your information in certain ways;
  • object to us using your information for our legitimate interests or for marketing;
  • ask us to transfer your information to you or another organisation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email us at contact@globalventurers.com. We will reply within one month. There is normally no charge.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of each email.

11. How long we keep your information

  • Booking records: six years after your journey ends, to meet tax and legal requirements.
  • Health and dietary information: deleted within three months of your journey ending, unless we need it for a claim or complaint.
  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a booking: up to two years.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.

12. How we keep your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure, password-protected systems and limiting access to people who need it. If a data breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner’s Office as the law requires.

13. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites and services, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or our payment provider. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they use your information.

14. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make our website work, for example to remember your language and your cookie choice. We also use Google Analytics cookies, which are statistical cookies only, to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We don’t use cookies for advertising. As UK law allows for this kind of analytics, they are on by default, and you can switch them off at any time using .
